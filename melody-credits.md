# Muted trumpet
Recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell for VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition.
Sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. The recordings are released under CC0.
Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/tree/440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e/Brass/Trumpet/harmonM-sus

The six soft sustained Harmon-muted trumpet takes are folded to mono, downsampled to 22.05 kHz and crossfaded for sustained notes. Their sounding pitches are B♭3, C4, A♭4, D5, F5 and A5.
